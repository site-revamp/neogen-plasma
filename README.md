NeoGen Plasma – Homepage Redesign
1. Website Overview
NeoGen Plasma is a medical technology brand operated by Energist Limited, specialising in nitrogen plasma technology for non-surgical skin regeneration. Its products, including NeoGen EVO and NeoGen PULSE, serve medical practitioners, aesthetic professionals, dermatology clinics, distributors and patients seeking advanced skin treatments.

The redesigned homepage focuses on positioning NeoGen as an innovative medical technology brand, communicating its plasma technology, treatment applications and clinical value while providing clearer pathways for practitioner enquiries, patient information and international business opportunities.
2. Main Improvements
1. Clearer Technology Positioning and Value Proposition
The homepage places greater emphasis on NeoGen's nitrogen plasma technology and its role in skin regeneration. The product technology and intended treatment benefits are communicated more clearly, helping visitors understand what makes NeoGen different and why it is relevant to medical and aesthetic professionals.
2. Improved Product and Treatment Information
NeoGen EVO, NeoGen PULSE and their associated treatment applications receive clearer organisation and presentation. This creates a more structured information hierarchy, making it easier for practitioners and prospective patients to explore the technology, products and available clinical information.
3. Stronger Audience Segmentation and Enquiry Pathways
The homepage provides clearer direction for different audiences, including medical practitioners, aesthetic clinics, patients and international distributors. Practitioner enquiries, product demonstrations, training information and patient clinic-finder access are given clearer context, helping visitors identify the information relevant to their needs.
3. Design Improvements
- Visual Presentation: A refined medical technology aesthetic reinforces NeoGen's positioning in advanced skin regeneration.
- Layout: Improved content hierarchy separates technology information, product presentations, treatment applications and audience-specific content.
- Typography: Consistent typography creates a more professional and readable interface.
- User Experience: Clearer navigation and audience-focused content make it easier to explore products and relevant information.
- Content Consistency: Product names, technology explanations and business messaging follow a more cohesive presentation.
- Overall Appearance: The redesigned homepage establishes a more unified brand experience, balancing medical credibility, technological innovation and treatment-focused communication.
