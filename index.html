A different way to think about skin renewal.
The science made understandable

Your skin.
Renewed from within.

NeoGen converts nitrogen gas into plasma and delivers its thermal energy in controlled pulses. This stimulates a natural response within the skin, supporting remodelling and the production of collagen and elastin.

The aim is not simply to treat the surface. It is to work with the skin's own regenerative processes. Treatment intensity and suitability are assessed by the practitioner, based on the individual and the intended outcome.

A considered approach to skin quality

One technology.
Multiple dimensions of skin renewal.

NeoGen is designed to support several visible aspects of skin quality. A qualified practitioner can advise whether a treatment is appropriate for your skin and goals.

Firmness

Supports improvements in the appearance of skin laxity and firmness.

Lines

Can improve the appearance of fine lines and wrinkles, depending on treatment.

Tone

Supports a more even-looking skin tone and the appearance of pigmentation.

Texture

Designed to improve the look and feel of uneven or rough skin texture.

Radiance

Supports improvements in overall skin quality and the appearance of radiance.

Personalisation

Treatment intensity and planning can be tailored by a trained practitioner.
